## Code and ideas adapted from PureCutCNC

The machine definition files in `src/core/machines/` (grblHAL, LinuxCNC, Mach3, UCCNC, Generic) and the layout `src/core/machines.ts` reads are adapted from the machine definitions of **PureCutCNC** (https://github.com/PureCutCNC/purecutcnc), which is licensed under the **Apache License, version 2.0**. PureCutCNC's notice reads:

> PureCutCNC (https://github.com/PureCutCNC)
> Copyright 2026 Franja (Frank) Povazanj
>
> This product includes software developed by Franja (Frank) Povazanj as part of the PureCutCNC project.

The Apache licence text is in `licenses/PureCutCNC-Apache-2.0.txt`. Apache-2.0 material may be included in an LGPL-3.0-or-later project.

What was changed from their files: the "generated by PureCutCNC" and date comment lines were removed (open-cam writes its own header), the pause command is not read (see `docs/DEVELOPER.md`, "Machine definitions"), operation headers, canned cycles, coolant and rotary settings are not used, and a block of open-cam settings (`openCam`) was added. Marlin and GRBL definitions were written for open-cam in the same layout.

The **setup sheet** (`src/setupsheet.ts`) is open-cam's own code; the idea of a printable per-job report was taken from PureCutCNC's operation booklet.
