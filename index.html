open-cam Help & guide

The automatic backup is paused until you choose.

1. Your drawing

SVG or DXF. Units are read from the file; layers are kept.
Import settings
Changing these re-reads the file and clears your operations.
Add text
The text becomes ordinary lines on a "Text" layer, so you can cut around it, pocket it or engrave along it. Leave X/Y empty to put it in the middle of the view.
3D model (STL)
Load a 3D model to carve its top surface. Choose the bit under "Bit" below: a ball nose for finishing.
Rough first with a flat bit, then finish with a ball nose. Only the top surface is cut: anything under an overhang is ignored.

2. Machine & material

Advanced: my own speeds
Starts from the chosen material. Tick the box, then change the numbers for a material that is not listed.
The router is manual: the file pauses so you can switch it on (and again at bit changes).

Two-sided job (optional)

Select the front outline shape and click the first button; then the matching back outline and click the second. They line up the two sides.

Long parts - split into segments (optional)

The stock is slid along between segments and located on dowels, so each piece lines up with the last. Each segment re-cuts the overlap so tiny alignment errors do not leave a step. If you also turn the stock over, the same dowels register the flip.

Dowels & registration

Hole depth is worked out for you from the dowel, stock and spoilboard. "Max hole depth" is the deepest your setup can drill (bit length or Z travel); leave it empty to use the bit's cutting length. 3 keyed = one dowel at one end, a pair at the other, so the stock only fits one way round after the turn-over. With 2 or 4 dowels the stock can be seated rotated or flipped the wrong way. Mark the top of the stock before side 1 so you know which face was up. Dowel holes are drilled first in side 1 and sit on the turn-over line so they line up again once the stock is flipped.

3. Operations

Nothing selected - click lines in the drawing
For V-carving, Depth is the deepest the tip will go. Thin strokes are shallower automatically, so the cut is wider where the shape is wider.
For inlay, Depth is the depth of the recess. The program first cuts the recess, pauses so you can swap in the insert board, then cuts the insert (mirrored, so it reads correctly once flipped over).
Zero X/Y at the bottom-left of your work area and Z on the TOP of the material. Your part is placed 5 mm in from that corner.
Click = select/deselect · Shift+drag = box select · Drag = pan · Wheel = zoom
3D simulation
Drag to rotate · right-drag or Shift+drag to move · wheel to zoom. Light wood = uncut top, darker = deeper, grey = cut right through.
